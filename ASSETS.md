# Artwork and media

Music and associated artwork remain the property of their respective rights holders. This app does not bundle or claim ownership of recordings. Catalogue preview URLs and full-track links are supplied by Apple's public music search service.

## Generated editorial art

- `public/images/discovery-hero.jpg`: AI-generated fictional concert musician, created for the discovery banner. This image does not depict or identify a real featured artist.
- `public/images/playlist-{love,night,sunset,party}.webp`: AI-generated editorial mood artwork, created for the app's curated playlists.
- `public/icon.svg` and PNG variants: original application mark.
